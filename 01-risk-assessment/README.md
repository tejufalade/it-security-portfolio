# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Overview

This project is a self-directed cybersecurity risk assessment of a fictional UK-based online retailer.

The purpose of the exercise was to apply cybersecurity concepts learned through independent study to a realistic business scenario.

## Organisation

**Company:** Northstar Retail Ltd

**Employees:** 50

**Industry:** Online retail

### Technology Environment

- Microsoft 365
- Windows laptops
- Cloud storage
- Customer database
- E-commerce website
- Corporate Wi-Fi
- HR/payroll system
- Remote workers

## Objectives

The assessment aims to:

1. Identify important business assets
2. Identify relevant threats
3. Identify vulnerabilities
4. Assess likelihood and impact
5. Prioritise cybersecurity risks
6. Recommend appropriate controls

## Key Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phishing | High | High | Critical |
| Ransomware | Medium | High | High |
| Lost laptop | Medium | High | High |
| Weak passwords | High | Medium | High |
| Excessive privileges | Medium | High | High |
| Unpatched software | Medium | High | High |
| Data leakage | Medium | High | High |
| Third-party compromise | Medium | High | High |

## Recommended Controls

### Phishing

- Multi-factor authentication
- Security awareness training
- Email filtering
- Phishing simulations

### Ransomware

- Endpoint protection
- Regular backups
- Patch management
- Network segmentation

### Lost Laptop

- Full-disk encryption
- Device management
- Remote wipe capability
- Strong authentication

## Framework

The recommendations were considered against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework:

- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

## Key Learning

This project helped me understand how cybersecurity risks can be assessed from both a technical and business perspective.
